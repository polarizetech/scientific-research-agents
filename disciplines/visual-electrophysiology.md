## Visual neuroscience and electrophysiology

The ocular optical path, retinal transduction and circuitry, ascending visual pathways, visual cortex, and
ERG/VEP/SSVEP measurement.

For a visual-pathway trace, visual evoked potential, optical-to-neural transformation, or visual stimulus
design, use the `visual-evoked-potentials` specialist profile first. It owns the retrieval-first evidence
ledger, definitions of optical/stimulus/neural phase, stage-by-stage response format, and adversarial
controls.

- **Keep levels separate.** Optical fields, retinal irradiance, photoreceptor currents, retinal population
  signals, spikes, scalp potentials, source estimates, and percepts are not interchangeable. [BK]
- **Stimuli.** Report spectrum, luminance or irradiance, contrast definition, spatial geometry, temporal
  waveform, viewing distance, field/eccentricity, adaptation, correction, display timing, and fixation. [BK]
- **Recording.** State modality, montage/reference, impedance, filters, sample rate, trigger timing, epoch
  rules, artefact handling, and whether harmonics were prespecified. [BK]
- **Safety.** Flicker, intensity, wavelength, duration, photosensitivity, and ocular-health exclusions require
  current authoritative limits and stop criteria. [BK]
